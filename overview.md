# fabnet overview

fabnet is a quiet atlas for finding tools and people who can make physical things nearby.

the main map answers a practical question: where can i make this? it brings public makerspaces, fabrication labs, equipped libraries, and campus workshops into one readable view. each place includes the equipment, access conditions, and source information someone needs before visiting.

local network answers the related question: who near me can make this? approved makers can describe their printers and fabrication tools without publishing a home address. people can submit a job, compare nearby capabilities, and arrange local pickup, delivery, or shipping.

fabnet is for students, hobbyists, designers, repairers, small teams, and anyone who needs access to fabrication without already knowing the local landscape. it avoids feeds, follower counts, rankings, and noisy marketplace behavior. the experience stays focused on place, capability, availability, and trust.
