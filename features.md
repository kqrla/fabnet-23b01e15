# features

## public fabrication atlas

- browse public makerspaces, fabrication labs, and libraries by city.
- filter places by fabrication capability.
- search supported postcode areas and move the map to that area.
- open compact previews and detailed drawers for access, equipment, and source information.
- switch among restrained visual themes without changing the underlying data.

## student mode

- choose a university or community college in the active city.
- show reviewed campus fabrication resources for that institution.
- read access restrictions, equipment capabilities, and the supporting source.

## suggestions

- suggest a new place or correction from the map.
- preserve the source address and context needed for later review.

## local network

- browse approved nearby makers by approximate service area.
- inspect multiple machines, materials, supplies, capabilities, availability, and fulfillment options.
- search by postcode without exposing a maker's exact home address.
- submit a fabrication request with job details and matching preferences.
- preview maker and requester dashboards without creating an account.
- maintain a pending maker profile before administrator approval.
- open an optional public maker page after approval.

## public information pages

- use the landing, about, and features pages to understand fabnet before opening the map.
- discover indexable city pages through the generated sitemap.
